This policy describes AE Studio, the desktop application distributed by AE Education, LTD. Contact: byeollae.apple.english@gmail.com.

Local files and preferences

AE Studio processes documents and projects on your computer. Preferences, recent paths, exports and optional local helper data are stored in your Windows user profile. Choosing an online feature can transmit the content you explicitly submit to its provider. AE Studio does not operate a central document-storage or analytics service.

Google Drive data

With your consent, AE Studio uses the Google Drive drive.file permission to access files you select for the app and files it creates. The app uses your account identity, file names, identifiers, metadata and selected file contents to show your connection, list permitted files, download or convert them, and upload new copies that you choose. It does not request access to your entire Drive. Drive content is not automatically submitted to AI services.

AE Studio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, transfer it to data brokers, or use it to develop or train general-purpose AI models.

Account storage and optional AI

AE Studio protects its Google Drive and ChatGPT connection credentials with Windows account encryption. Official Claude and Google Antigravity helpers manage their own sign-in data in separate local folders; their Windows storage may include unencrypted credential files. Protect your Windows account and never share the personal data folder.

When you submit a prompt, its text is sent to the selected AI provider. Each provider applies its own privacy policy, account terms and usage controls. AE Studio does not automatically buy credits or fall back to a paid API key. Existing provider credit settings can still affect charges, so review them before requesting generation. Local ComfyUI generation runs on your computer after its runtime and model downloads.

Sharing and retention

We do not receive your local files or connection tokens through an AE Studio backend. Providers receive data needed for the actions you choose. Downloaded files, exports, helper data and local account records remain on your computer until you remove them. Disconnecting removes the app's local connection data; it does not delete your Drive files or local exports. You can also revoke access in your Google Account. Uninstalling preserves personal data and documents.

Other services and this website

Microsoft, Google, OpenAI, Anthropic, GitHub and other linked services have their own policies. Downloads and optional provider helpers may contact their publishers. This informational website uses no advertising or analytics scripts added by AE Education; its hosting provider may process ordinary request logs.

Your choices

Cloud accounts are optional. You can use local editing, cancel sign-in, disconnect accounts, revoke provider consent, delete local exports or request help removing local data. Do not send sensitive files to support unless needed and agreed. Children should use account connections only with an authorized adult and in accordance with provider age requirements.

Changes

We will update this page when the app's data practices change and show the effective date above.
