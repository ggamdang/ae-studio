Windows desktop preview

One home for your creative work.

AE Studio brings document, spreadsheet, presentation, PDF, photo, vector, layout, art, video and motion workspaces into one desktop window.

Built by AE Education, LTD., with open-source components and the APPLE English brand. Local editing works without a cloud account. Optional connections let you use Google Drive and supported AI services with your own account.

Your files. Your choice.

Choose a file, work in its matching workspace, then save an editable project or export a copy. Windows file-opening options are optional. The app keeps your existing defaults until you change them.

Google Drive

AE Studio requests access only to files you select for the app or create with it. You can import supported files and upload a new copy. Signing in does not upload your local folders or send Drive content to AI.

Preview availability

This is an unsigned Windows 10/11 x64 preview. Obtain the installer and corresponding source from AE Education. Some formats require conversion and advanced Office or Adobe features may not be preserved. Keep original files and review exports before use.

Optional cloud services have their own eligibility, usage limits and terms. Local image generation requires a separate, large ComfyUI add-on and suitable hardware. No paid subscription is required for ordinary local editing.
